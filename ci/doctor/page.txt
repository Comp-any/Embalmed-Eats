Wax dipping: in the crafting grid with 1 Beeswax per food
Resin lacquer: in the crafting grid with 1 Resin per food
Formaldehyde: seal it in a barrel with 0.25 L of formaldehyde solution per food, for 48 hours
Salt and lime cure: seal it in a barrel with 1 curing salt per food, for 120 hours

== Eating preserved food ==
From toxicity 1: queasy (loses 30% hunger, 0 poison damage, nausea 0, 0% slower)

From toxicity 4: severe (loses 100% hunger, 12 poison damage, nausea 1, 30% slower)
From toxicity 5: lethal (loses 100% hunger, 40 poison damage, nausea 1.1, 50% slower)
Each preserved food eaten adds 1; one point wears off every 8 in-game hours; it stops at 10.
